Nub Agent privacy policy

Last updated 14 August 2026. This covers the Nub Agent Android app and the matching Mac desktop app.

Who we are

Nub Agent is published by MaiaVM. Contact: privacy@maiavm.com.

What the app stores on the device

After you link the phone with Telegram, the app keeps a session on the device: the Maia host URL, the instance id, and a desktop session token. It does not store your Telegram password, a VM gateway token, or the one-time pairing code after pairing finishes.

Unlink this phone (Settings) deletes that local session. Android backup of app data is turned off.

What is sent over the network

  • Pairing start/poll requests to maiavm.com so Telegram can approve the phone.
  • Chat messages you send, plus conversation list/state, to your Maia VM through maiavm.com.

Traffic uses HTTPS. The app does not include advertising or third-party analytics SDKs.

Account

Your account is the Telegram identity you use with the Maia bot. Subscription and billing stay in Telegram. We do not process payments inside the Android app.

Your choices

  • Do not link the phone — preview mode stays on the device and does not send chats to a VM.
  • Unlink this phone to drop the local session token.
  • Ask us at privacy@maiavm.com to delete server-side pairing/session records tied to your Telegram account.

Children

Nub Agent is not directed at children under 13.
